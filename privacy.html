Privacy Policy

Alarmeetings · Effective September 26, 2026

Alarmeetings turns your calendar meetings into alarms. It's built to do that entirely on your iPhone. Alarmeetings doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Calendar

With your permission, Alarmeetings reads events from the next seven days of your calendars to schedule alarms. Event details, such as titles, times, locations and meeting links, are only used on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Location

Location access is optional. If you allow it, Alarmeetings uses your current location to estimate travel time to meetings that have a street address, so the alarm can ring when it's time to leave. To calculate the route, your location and the meeting's address are sent to Apple Maps, which handles them under Apple's privacy policy. Alarmeetings keeps your last known location on your device for up to 12 hours so background syncs can reuse it.

Notifications and alarms

Alarms and reminder notifications are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, the list of upcoming alarms and any changes you make to individual alarms are stored on your iPhone and shared only with the Alarmeetings widget. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Alarmeetings isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
