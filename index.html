Common questions

A meeting didn't get an alarm

Alarms are only scheduled for meetings on the days and hours set in Settings, Schedule. The default is Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. All-day events, declined invitations and cancelled events never get alarms. To ring for one meeting outside your schedule, open it on the Alarms tab and turn its alarm on.

My alarms are out of date

iOS doesn't let apps run on a schedule, so Alarmeetings syncs whenever it can: when you open it, when your calendar changes while it's running, when you stop an alarm and during Background App Refresh. Keep Background App Refresh on for Alarmeetings in iOS Settings, and open the app if you get a reminder that it hasn't synced.

How do I change how early the alarm rings?

Go to Settings, Alarm and change the number of minutes. To set a different time for a single meeting, open it on the Alarms tab and pick a custom alarm time.

Why does the alarm ring earlier for some meetings?

When an event has a street address and you've allowed location access, Alarmeetings estimates the travel time and rings when it's time to leave. You can turn this off in Settings, Travel time, or change how you get there for any meeting.

How do I join a call from the alarm?

When a meeting has a video link, the alarm shows an Open button that opens the link.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add the Alarmeetings Upcoming Alarms widget. Settings, Widget setup has a short video.

How do I turn off every alarm?

Turn off Alarms enabled at the top of Settings. Every alarm is cancelled until you turn it back on.
